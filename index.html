Today's mission

    For grown-ups

    Activities follow typical Oxford Class 1 topics used in Pakistani schools: phonics and letter sounds, sight words, days of the week, opposites, masculine and feminine words, counting to 20, addition and subtraction within 10, number order, comparing numbers and 2D shapes. Check her school book list to match the exact units.

    • Sindhi words are shown with English-letter spelling to help her say them. Please check them with her Sindhi teacher; the voice reads the English spelling, not true Sindhi sounds. Words the translator looks up beyond the word cards come from Claude.
    • If she gets a question wrong twice, a "Show me how" box teaches an easy way to work it out.
    • Every 3 stars fills a gift box. Some boxes hold a prize coupon (a dessert, a park trip, cartoon time). Please honour them!
    • Every question has 🔊 Urdu and 🔊 Sindhi buttons that read it aloud while the screen stays in English. They use the device's Urdu or Hindi voice, so Sindhi is read the way a Hindi voice would say it.
    • After 20 minutes of play, a break reminder pops up.
    • Videos stay only on the device she records them on. Nothing is uploaded.
    • Stars and prizes are saved in this browser.

    🎙️ Voice & speed

    Pick the voice Mishka likes best. Tap Try to hear it.

    Want a sweeter voice?
    • iPad / iPhone: Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › English. Download Serena (Enhanced) or Kate (Enhanced). For Urdu and Sindhi, also download Lekha under Hindi.
    • Android: Settings › Accessibility › Text-to-speech. Choose Google, then install voice data for English (UK) and Hindi or Urdu.
    • Windows: open the app in Microsoft Edge for its natural voices (Libby, Sonia, Swara).
    • Mac: System Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › System voice › Manage voices. Add Serena, Kate or Lekha.

    After adding a voice, close and reopen the app.
