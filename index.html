    For grown-ups: what these train
    • Stop and think (Fruit or Bug, Day & Night) trains self-control: holding back a quick wrong reaction.
    • Memory (What's Missing, Firefly, Copy Me) trains working memory: keeping things in mind while you use them.
    • Switch Sort trains flexible thinking: changing to a new rule. Find It Fast and Listen & Count train attention.
    • Calm Bubble slow breathing helps children settle before homework or after a busy day.
    • A few minutes a day works best. Her best scores appear on each tile.

    🎙️ Voice & speed
    
    
    Pick the voice Mishka likes best. Tap Try to hear it.
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    Want a sweeter voice?
      
      
        
    • iPad / iPhone: Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › English. Download Serena (Enhanced) or Kate (Enhanced). For Urdu and Sindhi, also download Lekha under Hindi.
      • 
        
    • Android: Settings › Accessibility › Text-to-speech. Choose Google, then install voice data for English (UK) and Hindi or Urdu.
      • 
        
    • Windows: open the app in Microsoft Edge for its natural voices (Libby, Sonia, Swara).
      • 
        
    • Mac: System Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › System voice › Manage voices. Add Serena, Kate or Lekha.
      • 
      
    
      
    After adding a voice, close and reopen the app.
